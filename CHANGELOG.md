# Changelog

## 0.2.0

### Added
- `forloop.every_weekday()`, `every_weekend()`, `every_week(on=...)` and
  `on_days([...])` for loops on chosen days of the week at a set time.
- `ask.password()`: hidden typing, with optional `confirm=True` and `min_length`.
- `say.spinner()`: a spinning animation for a `with` block that ends with ✔ or ✘.
- `files.read_csv()`, `write_csv()` and `append_csv()`.

## 0.1.0

First release: `forloop`, `loopvars`, `wait`, `ask`, `say`, `files`, `safe` and `rand`.
